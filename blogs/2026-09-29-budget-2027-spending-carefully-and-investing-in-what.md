By Senator Alan Maclean, Minister for Treasury and Resources Budget 2027 marks a change of direction in the way we manage Jersey’s public finances. Public spending has risen substantially in recent years. Some of that additional expenditure was necessary and has delivered important benefits, but higher spending has not always produced better outcomes.
