On 10 September 2026, Jersey’s Social Security system marks its 75th anniversary. For three quarters of a century, it has supported Islanders through sickness, parenthood, disability, bereavement and old age. Today it is an established part of Island life, but its introduction was one of Jersey’s most significant and debated social reforms.
